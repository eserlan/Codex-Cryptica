# The Shelf

Built a monster you want in another campaign? A stash of magic items worth reusing? The Shelf carries entities from one vault into another without anything leaving the app.

## Putting something on the Shelf

Three ways in:

- Open an entity and click **Send to Shelf** in its header
- Select several entities in the **graph**, right-click, and choose **Send to Shelf**
- Do the same from the **table**

Shelving only reads — nothing in the vault you shelved from is changed.

## Bringing it into another vault

Switch to the vault you want the entity in, open the **Shelf** from the sidebar, tick the entries you want, and choose **Import into this vault**.

Entries stay on the Shelf after you import them, so the same monster can go into several campaigns.

## What travels with an entity

Everything you wrote: its description and lore, labels, aliases, dates, images, sound bites, and its stat sheet — including the stat sheet template and its presentation, so the sheet looks the same in its new home.

Links between entities travel too, with a couple of sensible limits:

- If you shelve a group together, links **between those entities** are rebuilt.
- A link pointing at something already in the destination reconnects by name.
- If nothing matches, or more than one thing does, the link is left off and listed for you afterwards. It won't guess — a link quietly attached to the wrong entity is far harder to notice than one that's missing.

## When names clash

Importing never overwrites anything. If the destination already has an entity with the same title, the arriving one gets a number after it, and you're told which was renamed.

If both vaults have a stat sheet template that differs, you're asked which to use before anything is written. Choosing the shelved one adds it alongside what you already have rather than replacing it.

## Where the Shelf lives

**In this browser, and nowhere else.** That matters:

- It is **not a backup**. Use vault backups for that.
- It **cannot send an entity to another person**, or to your other devices.
- Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Think of it as a workbench you carry things across on, not a place to keep them. The Shelf shows how much space it's using, and you can remove single entries or clear it entirely at any time.
