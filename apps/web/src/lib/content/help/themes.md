# Themes

Codex Cryptica allows you to shift the visual dimension of your workspace using **Themes**. World genre themes change the colours, typography and textures of world content. **App Appearance** separately controls the neutral interface around it, such as navigation and settings.

## Changing Themes

You can switch between available templates at any time:

1.  Open **Settings** (click the gear icon or press `S`).
2.  Navigate to the **Theme** tab.
3.  Choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark** under **App Appearance** for the interface.
4.  Select a theme under **World Genre Theme** for your campaign content.

Changes apply immediately. World genre themes affect content such as the graph and entity views; navigation and settings follow App Appearance.

## Available Themes

- **Ancient Parchment**: Our flagship fantasy theme, designed to feel like an enchanted magic tome with parchment textures and inked typography.
- **Sci-Fi Terminal**: A high-contrast, neon-green terminal aesthetic for futuristic settings.
- **Blood & Noir**: A dark, moody theme with crimson accents, ideal for horror or investigative campaigns.
- **Cosmic Horror**: Sea-green ink, impossible geometry, and deep-sea unease for investigations into the unknown. Unlike Blood & Noir, it focuses on alien scale and forbidden knowledge—not vampires, bloodlines, or gothic-noir intrigue.
- **Neon Night**: A vibrant, cyberpunk-inspired palette with high-energy colors.
- **Wasteland**: A rugged, textured theme for post-apocalyptic or industrial worlds.
- **Workspace**: A neutral style for general-purpose worldbuilding.

## Persistence

Your selected theme is saved to your local browser storage and will be automatically applied every time you return to the workspace.
