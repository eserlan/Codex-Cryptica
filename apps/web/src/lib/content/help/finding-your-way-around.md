# Finding your way around

The **Activity Bar** is the strip of icon buttons that runs down the left side of the screen on a computer. On a phone it runs along the bottom. On a computer, hover over a button to see what it is called. This page lists every button by that name.

The **top bar** across the top of the screen holds search, **Create**, your vault and Settings. It is described under [The top bar](#the-top-bar) below.

The Activity Bar has two kinds of button:

- **Views** take you to a different screen. The button for the screen you are on is highlighted.
- **Tools** open something beside or on top of the screen you are on, such as a panel. Some tools close when clicked again. The Session Journal button opens or focuses its tab; click **Notes** to close the window.

Next to the vault name you may also see:

- **DEMO MODE**, with **Make this mine** to keep the demo world as your own vault, and **Exit demo**.
- **OFFLINE**, when you have no connection. Your data stays available, but cloud features and the Lore Oracle wait until you are back online.
- **No Vault Open**, when no vault is loaded. Its menu has **Explore Demo Vault**, **Create New Vault** and **Open Existing Vault**.

### In a shared world

The top bar is smaller. It shows a **READ-ONLY GUEST** badge, **Voice** during a live session, **Explore Worlds**, **Exit Guest Mode**, and **Help and legal information** (the question-mark icon).

### On a phone

The top bar only keeps the menu button, **Open Die Roller**, the **CC** name and **Search**. Everything else is in the **menu drawer**. Tap the three-line **Toggle menu** button to open it. It has these sections:

- **Mode**: the Guided and Full Toolbox switch.
- **Views** and **Tools**: every Activity Bar button, including the ones the bottom bar has no room for.
- **Vault**: your vault's name, **Manage vault** and **Save**.
- **Application**: **Settings**.
- **Links**: Explore, Help, Privacy and Terms.

## Why a button might be missing

- **On a phone**, the bottom bar only has room for the main buttons: Graph, Map, Canvas, Timeline, Table, Entities and Oracle. Play, Rolls & Decks, Biomes, Generators, Shelf, Notes, Session Journal and Chat are only in the **menu drawer**. On a computer they are all in the bar.
- **In Guided Mode**, the top bar hides the Die Roller, the status icons, **Manage vault**, **Save** and **Explore public worlds**.
- **Generators** only appear once your vault is ready for them.
- **Chat** is hidden when AI is switched off for the vault.
- **In a shared, read-only world** you will not see Generators, Shelf, Notes or the Session Journal, because they change things in a vault you own. The Graph button opens the shared world.

## Things that look similar

- **Table** is the Entity Table, an overview of all your entries. **Rolls & Decks** is for random tables you roll on. They are different things.
- **Entities** opens a panel beside your current screen. **Table** takes you to a full screen of its own.
- **Notes** is a scratchpad. **Session Journal** is the running record of a session. Both live in the same QuickNote window.
