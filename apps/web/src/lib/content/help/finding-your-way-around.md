# Finding your way around

The **Activity Bar** is the strip of icon buttons that runs down the left side of the screen on a computer. On a phone it runs along the bottom. On a computer, hover over a button to see what it is called. This page lists every button by that name.

Buttons come in two kinds:

- **Views** take you to a different screen. The button for the screen you are on is highlighted.
- **Tools** open something beside or on top of the screen you are on, such as a panel. Click the button again to close it.

## Views

| Button            | What it opens                                                                        | Read more                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Graph**         | The Knowledge Graph: every entry as a node, with its connections drawn between them. | [Knowledge Graph](/help#help/graph-basics)                                            |
| **Map**           | The World Map, for placing locations and running a session on a map.                 | [Map Mode](/help#help/map-mode)                                                       |
| **Canvas**        | The Spatial Canvas: a free-form board where you arrange entries and notes.           | [Spatial Canvas](/help#help/spatial-canvas)                                           |
| **Timeline**      | The World Chronology: your entries placed on the calendar of your world.             | [Chronology](/help#help/chronology)                                                   |
| **Table**         | The Entity Table: every entry in one list that you can filter and sort.              | [Entity Table](/help#help/entity-table)                                               |
| **Play**          | Solo play. Start a solo session, or let the Oracle run an adventure.                 | [Solo Sessions](/help#help/solo-session), [Adventure Mode](/help#help/adventure-mode) |
| **Rolls & Decks** | Your random tables and card decks, where you roll and draw.                          | [Roll Tables & Decks](/help#help/random-tables-decks)                                 |

## Why a button might be missing

- **On a phone**, the bar only has room for the main buttons: Graph, Map, Canvas, Timeline, Table, Entities and Oracle. Play, Rolls & Decks, Generators, Shelf, Notes, Session Journal and Chat are in the **menu drawer** instead. On a computer they are all in the bar.
- **Generators** only appear once your vault is ready for them.
- **Chat** is hidden when AI is switched off for the vault.
- **In a shared, read-only world** you will not see Generators, Shelf, Notes or the Session Journal, because they change things in a vault you own. The Graph button opens the shared world.

## Things that look similar

- **Table** is the Entity Table, an overview of all your entries. **Rolls & Decks** is for random tables you roll on. They are different things.
- **Entities** opens a panel beside your current screen. **Table** takes you to a full screen of its own.
- **Notes** is a scratchpad. **Session Journal** is the running record of a session. Both live in the same QuickNote window.
