## What a solo session is

A solo session is for one person who is both game master and player. It gathers the tools you use while playing alone in one place: a map with SOLO fog, quick dice, your Session Journal, notes and the Oracle. Nothing in it needs AI. Your dice, tables and notes work the same way with AI turned off.

## Start a solo session

1. Choose **Play** in the activity bar.
2. Choose **Start Solo Session**.
3. Pick a map. The map you had open last is chosen for you. Choose **No map** to start without one.
4. Keep **Start a Session Journal** on to record your session. If a journal is already running, the option offers to continue it.
5. Choose **Start**.

The map opens with **SOLO** on, so fogged areas stay hidden from you. Fog must be on for SOLO to hide anything. If the map has fog off, turn fog on first.

Starting a session does not turn on Adventure Mode, and the Oracle does not act as game master.

## The solo bar

While a session runs, a bar sits under the header on every screen. It shows the map name and the current scene, then:

- **Quick roll**: type a dice expression and press Enter.
- **More dice** opens the full dice window for tables, decks and history.
- **Ask Oracle** opens the Oracle beside the screen. It appears only when AI is on.
- **Journal** opens your Session Journal.
- **Map** goes back to your session's map. With no map, **Choose map** picks one.
- **Add note** opens a quick note.
- **End session** ends the session.

Use the chevron to minimise the bar to a single button. The bar stays minimised on this device until you open it again. On a phone, the bar is one button that opens the same tools in a sheet.

## Quick roll

Type an expression such as `d20` or `2d6+1`, then press Enter. The result shows in the bar, and the map and the screen you are on do not change. Nothing pops up.

Each roll is added to your roll history, the same as a roll in the dice window, and to your Session Journal if one is running. An expression the app cannot read shows a short message, and nothing is rolled or recorded.

Press Enter on an empty box to roll the last expression again.

## Scenes

Type a name in the scene box and choose **New scene** to start a scene. While a journal is running, each new scene starts a new section in the journal, so the record reads scene by scene. Choose **Rename** to change the current scene's name, and the section is renamed too.

Scene names are kept on this device and survive a reload.

## Resume a session

Your session is kept across reloads and across switching vaults. Open **Play** again and choose **Resume Solo Session** to return to your map. Resuming does not change any map setting.

## End a session

Choose **End session** in the bar. If a journal is running, you can end it too, or keep it running. Either way, nothing is deleted: your journal entries, rolls, map, map settings and vault stay as they are.

## Solo means solo

While a solo session is running, you cannot share your vault or turn on Player View. Those controls explain that you need to end the solo session first. In the same way, you cannot start a solo session while shared play is on. End shared play first.

## Asking for help

Cif answers questions about how Codex Cryptica works, including Play and the solo bar. Questions about what happens in your story belong to the [Oracle](/help#help/oracle-guide), or to your own tables and prep.
