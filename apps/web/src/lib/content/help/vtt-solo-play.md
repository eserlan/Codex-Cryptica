## Play a map on your own

Solo map play is for one person acting as both GM and player. You stay in GM view, so the tools for preparing and changing the map remain available. The **SOLO** setting makes fogged areas look completely hidden on your device.

## Turn on concealment

### Can't see anything on your map?

If you cannot see anything on the map while playing alone, check the fog settings first. In GM view, turn **FOG: ON** and **SOLO: ON** to conceal unrevealed terrain and tokens; reveal the starting area with the brush or **Reveal hex**. Fog hides the map content until an area is revealed, so confirm the area is revealed before changing token visibility. **SOLO** is available only to the GM and only while fog is on. If the map still looks empty, turn **SOLO: OFF** to compare the normal GM view and check the map's layers.

1. Open the map in GM view and choose **FOG: ON**.
2. Choose **SOLO: ON** beside the fog control.
3. Reveal areas as you explore. Use the fog brush or right-click a hex and choose **Reveal hex**. Hide an area again with the brush or **Hide hex**.

Terrain, tokens, pins, labels, notes and hex coordinates remain concealed until their area is revealed. A token or note marked **Hide from Guests** appears when its area is revealed: in solo play the fog itself provides concealment. Moving any token, changing layers, using tiles and other GM tools continue to work. SOLO has no effect while fog is off and is available only in GM view. For map fog and player previews, see [Fog, Player View & Solo Play](/help#help/vtt-fog-player-view).

## Explore by moving your party

Mark the party token as a **Vision Source (PC)**. On a hex map, the **Vision** slider is measured in whole hexes; zero reveals the party's current hex only. Moving the party reveals its sight as it travels. A move made by dragging reveals areas live and is recorded when you release the token. One completed move has one undo step; `Ctrl` + `Z` hides the areas revealed by that move.

The travel readout shows the last move and the total since you opened the map or last reset it. On a hex map it shows hexes and the map's distance units; on square or gridless maps it shows straight-line distance. Choose **Reset** to start a fresh tally. Tokens without a vision source still move normally but do not count as party travel.

## Keep an expedition record

Start a [Session Journal](/help#help/quicknote) to record completed party moves alongside dice rolls, table results and card draws. Map entries include distance, destination coordinates when those labels are enabled, and how many new hexes the move revealed. Open **Capture** in the journal header to switch off map moves, or any other kind of automatic entry, while keeping the rest on. Promote a useful entry to a vault note through the same journal actions as any other entry.

Travel totals are temporary and stay in this browser tab. The journal is saved with your local vault. Solo play does not send or measure your map activity.

Start a whole solo session from [Play](/help#help/solo-session), which turns SOLO on for the map you choose and keeps your dice and journal close at hand.
