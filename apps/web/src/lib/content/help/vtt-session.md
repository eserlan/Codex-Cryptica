## Tactical VTT Sessions from Your Maps

Codex Cryptica includes an integrated **Virtual Tabletop (VTT)** mode on your campaign maps. Turn it on to place tokens, track initiative, measure distance, work with tile decks, and share the live table with your players. It adds a layer on top of the map; your map image, pins and fog are not changed by turning it on or off.

### How to Start a VTT Session

1. Open **Maps** from the activity bar and choose the map you want to use.
2. Click the **VTT OFF** button in the map controls to switch it to **VTT ON**.
3. The **VTT Sidebar** opens on the right, and the map bar adds the player view, fog, vision, layer and grid controls.

### Explore and Combat modes

The top of the VTT Sidebar has two mode buttons:

- **Explore** (compass): the everyday mode for moving around, placing tokens and notes, and revealing the map.
- **Combat** (swords): shows the **Initiative** panel at the top of the sidebar, with the round counter and a **Next Turn** button.

Switching mode is shared with connected players. Combat mode does not lock movement to whose turn it is: players can still move their own tokens at any time. What the turn order controls is the **Next Turn** button, which only the GM or the owner of the token whose turn it is can press. See [Initiative, Combat & Encounters](/help#help/vtt-combat-initiative).

### The VTT Sidebar

From top to bottom:

- **Mode and tools**: Explore and Combat, **Add Token**, the map note button, and **Encounters**.
- **Initiative** (Combat mode only).
- **Tile Decks** and **Vault Entities** (GM only): tiles to draw onto the map, and characters, creatures and items to drag onto it as tokens.
- **Token details** for the selected token.
- **Share Campaign** (GM only): starts the live session your players join.

The VTT chat sits in its own sidebar beside it. Collapse either sidebar to give the map more room.

### Map controls

The bar along the bottom of the map has **PLAYER VIEW**, **FOG**, **VISION**, **LABELS**, the active **layer**, and the grid button. A ruler switch sits at the bottom left of the map. Arrow keys pan the map and `+` and `-` zoom. Press `Alt` + `Left` or `Right` arrow to turn the selected token.

### Pausing or Ending a Session

Click **VTT ON** to return to ordinary map mode. Token positions, revealed fog and encounter data stay in your local vault for next time. Use **Encounters** to save a named snapshot you can come back to.

### Cif

Click **Cif** in the map bar to ask how any of this works. It stays available when the map is maximized, and you can pop it out into its own window to keep the map full size.
