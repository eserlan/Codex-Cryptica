## Hosting a session

Hosting is peer to peer, straight from your browser. There are no player accounts.

1. Turn **VTT ON** for your map.
2. Choose **Share Campaign** (the share button at the bottom of the VTT Sidebar).
3. Choose **START LIVE SESSION**, then **COPY** the link.
4. Send the link to your players. They open it in their browser to join.

Keep the host tab open for as long as the session runs. When it closes, the session ends for everyone. Connecting players can still rely on network services to set up or relay the connection.

## What players see and can do

- Players see the map, the tokens shown to them, pings, the fog as it is revealed, the initiative list in Combat mode, and chat.
- A player can move only the tokens **assigned to them**, and press **Next Turn** only when it is their token's turn. The host assigns tokens with **Owner** in the token details.
- Players do not get the host's tools: Tile Decks, Vault Entities, token creation and Share are hidden for them.
- The host sees everyone connected in the owner list and on the initiative rows.

See [Tokens, Players & Permissions](/help#help/vtt-tokens) and [Fog, Player View & Solo Play](/help#help/vtt-fog-player-view).

## Chat and rolls

The VTT chat sits in its own sidebar. Type a message, or use `/roll` for a roll, for example `/roll 2d20kh1 + 5`. The button beside the message box opens the full roller. Chat and rolls are shared with everyone connected, and the clear button empties the chat. Chat is saved with an encounter snapshot.

## Voice chat

While a live session is running, the host presses **Start voice chat** in the header to open a voice channel. Players choose **Join voice chat** from their own header, and everyone can mute their microphone. The host's button changes to **End voice chat** to close it for everyone.

## Privacy

The session runs between your browser and your players' browsers. Your vault stays on your computer. Players receive what the session shows them, such as visible tokens and revealed fog, not your private notes.

## Phones and tablets

On a phone, the VTT Sidebar fills the screen and the map controls sit behind a button, so the map stays visible. Open the controls with that button.
