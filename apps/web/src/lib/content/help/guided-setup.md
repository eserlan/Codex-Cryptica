## What guided setup is

Guided setup walks you through one short task in your own vault, step by step, until you can see the result. You choose the goal:

- **Build a world**: add a place and a character, connect them, and see them on the Graph.
- **Write a story**: add a character and a place or a plot note, link them, and find one again with Search or the Explorer.
- **Prepare an RPG campaign**: prepare a character or location with a generator, save it, then add a second one for your table, for example with **Generate related**.

A small card shows the current step. A step is ticked off only when you really do it, for example when the new place is saved. You can do the steps in any order: the card always shows the first one you still need.

## Starting, pausing and stopping

- The first time you open Codex Cryptica, a small card asks what you would like to do first. Pick a goal, choose **Explore on my own**, or close it.
- To start later, or to switch goal, open **Help** and use **Guided setup** under **Getting Started**. **Continue** picks up where you stopped, and **Start over** begins again.
- **Show me** opens the right screen or points at the right control. It only runs when you tap it.
- **Back** shows an earlier step, **Skip** moves past a step, and **Stop guided setup** ends it straight away. Press **Escape** inside the card, or use the minimise button, to shrink it to a small pill.
- If you reload the page, the card comes back on the same step. When you come back another day, it stays out of the way until you choose **Continue**.

## Tips from Cif

Once guided setup has finished or you have left it, Cif can offer one small tip about something that suits the goal you chose. Tips are off until you turn them on in **Settings → AI → Show tips from Cif**. When guided setup ends, you are also asked once whether you would like occasional tips; either answer means you are not asked again.

- A tip shows as a small chip beside the Cif button. It shows at most once per visit.
- Nothing is sent until you tap the tip, and then Cif answers the question it names.
- Tips never appear while you are typing, in a dialog, or while a tour or Cif is open.
- Dismissing a tip removes it for good. Dismissing two tips on the same screen quiets that screen until you turn the setting off and on again.
- Tips never read your vault, and they are off when AI is turned off.

## What it never does

Guided setup never creates, changes or deletes anything in your vault. Everything in your vault afterwards is something you made yourself. It works without AI and offline, and it sends nothing anywhere. Your progress is kept on this device only, and holds which steps you finished, never your entry names or text.

## Cif and the Oracle

Cif is the guide to using Codex Cryptica: it explains where things are and how they work. Some steps offer **Ask Cif**, which asks Cif a question about that step. It only appears when AI is turned on, and only sends a question when you tap it. The Oracle and the generators are different: they make ideas and content for your world.
