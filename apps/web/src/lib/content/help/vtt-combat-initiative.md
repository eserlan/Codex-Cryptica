## Combat mode and the initiative list

Choose **Combat** (the swords button) at the top of the VTT Sidebar. The **Initiative** panel appears with the current **Round** and a **Next Turn** button.

- **Add combatants**: select a token and choose **Add to Initiative** in its details.
- **Remove a combatant**: right-click its row (GM only). This removes the token from the map as well.
- **Set initiative**: type a number in the **Init** box on a row, or press the `Up` and `Down` arrow keys inside the box. The list re-sorts from highest to lowest, and keeps the turn on whoever's turn it was.
- **Reorder by hand**: drag a row to a new position, for example to break a tie your own way.
- **Ping a token**: use the radar button on a row, or double-click the row.
- **Look at a token**: the book button opens a linked entity, when you are allowed to see it.

## Turns and rounds

**Next Turn** moves to the next combatant. After the last one, the round counter goes up and the first combatant is up again. The active combatant is highlighted for everyone.

Only the GM, or the player who owns the token whose turn it is, can press **Next Turn**. The button is disabled for everyone else, and also while the list is empty.

Combat mode does not stop tokens moving out of turn. It shows initiative and the turn order. Players can still move their own tokens at any time, so agree at the table when movement is allowed.

## Initiative in its own window

Use **Pop out initiative list** at the top of the panel to open it in a separate window. Drag it to a second screen so the map stays full size.

## Encounter snapshots

Choose **Encounters** (the scroll button) to open **Encounter Snapshots**:

- **Encounter Name**: name what you are saving, for example "Goblin Ambush".
- **Save Current Encounter**: stores the table as it is now.
- **New Encounter**: clears tokens, initiative and chat for a fresh start.
- **Load**: restores a saved snapshot. **Delete** removes it after you confirm.

Each snapshot shows its name, round, token count and mode. A snapshot keeps the tokens and where they are, initiative order and values, the round and whose turn it is, the mode, the fog revealed during the session, the measurement and chat, and the grid settings and tile decks. It does not change your map or your vault entities.
