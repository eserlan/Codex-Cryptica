# Sharing Templates

You can share your own entity templates with other Codex Cryptica users, and install ones they have made. Open **Community templates** and switch to **Entity templates** to browse.

## Installing a template

Open a listing to read its description and see the note a new entity would start with. Choose **Install** to add a copy to your vault.

- The copy is one of **your own templates**. You can edit, duplicate, export or delete it like any other.
- It **never becomes your default** and it doesn't change any notes you already have.
- If you already have a template with the same name for that type, you're asked to pick a different name or cancel. Nothing is overwritten.
- Later changes to the listing, or its removal, don't change your copy.

## Publishing a template

In **Settings → Templates → Entity templates**, choose **Publish** on one of your own templates. Built-in templates and file templates can't be published directly: **Duplicate** one first and publish your copy.

You add a short description, at least one label (a genre or game system, such as "Fantasy" or "Pathfinder"), and, if you like, a display name to credit yourself. You then see exactly what will be shared before you confirm.

**What is shared:** the name, the type and the text of the template, plus your description, labels and display name. **The whole template text is public**, so check it before you publish. It never includes your notes. Your vault and everything else stay private.

By publishing you confirm you have the right to share the template and that others may copy, use and adapt it.

## Your owner token

When you publish, you get an **owner token**. Keep it somewhere safe. It's what lets you update or remove the listing if you clear this browser's data. You can copy it or save it as a file. It's shown only once, and it is never saved in your vault or in exported files.

If you lose access, open your listing, choose **I published this template**, and enter the token to get your controls back.

## Updating, unpublishing and deleting

- **Update listing** sends your template's current text and details. The listing keeps the same address.
- **Unpublish** hides the listing from the directory. You keep your own template and can **Republish** it later at the same address.
- **Delete permanently** removes the listing and its text from the directory for good. Your own template isn't changed.

Deleting your local template does **not** remove the public listing. Unpublish or delete the listing yourself.

## Reporting a template

If a listing is inappropriate, spam, or copied without permission, open it and choose **Report this template**. You can report a listing once. If the operator removes a listing, that removal is final: the token can't restore it, though you can publish the template again as a new listing.

## Good to know

- Sharing is optional. Nothing is uploaded unless you go through **Publish**.
- In a shared or read-only vault you can browse and preview listings, but you can't install or publish.
