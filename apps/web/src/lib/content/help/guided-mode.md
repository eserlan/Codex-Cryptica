# Guided Mode

Use the **Guided / Full Toolbox** switch in the app header (or the mobile menu) to choose how many tools the workspace shows. Guided mode starts enabled for new users. Your choice is saved in this browser and applies across worlds and sessions; it is not a setting stored in an individual vault.

## What changes in Guided Mode

Guided mode keeps the main workspace focused on your entities and common creation tasks. The **+ Create** action asks what you want to make: a character, place, faction, event, item, or a custom creation. If an entity is selected, the generated draft can use it as context. Choosing **Custom** opens the full generator setup so you can pick a generator yourself.

Some advanced header controls are hidden while Guided mode is on, including the die roller, vault actions menu, sync and voice controls, and the public-world explorer. Switch to **Full Toolbox** to show those controls and the full tool selection. The mode changes which controls are visible; it does not delete, rewrite, or hide campaign entities.

## Contextual suggestions

Guided mode can show a suggestion when an entity has a structural gap, such as a faction without a leader, a location without a settlement, or a threat without a responder. These suggestions are deterministic checks of your local entity connections; they do not call AI or change the vault on their own.

Choose the suggestion's action to open a generator with the related entity as context. The generator creates a draft for you to review; the new entity is added only if you save it. You can dismiss a suggestion without changing your entities. Cif can explain the suggestion and steps, but cannot follow it, save a draft, or change your vault for you.

## Quick Start World

Quick Start World is separate from Guided mode. From the empty workspace, open the vault switcher and choose **Quick Start World**. Choose a world genre and appearance, review the preview of four to six linked starter entities, and optionally enter a seed premise. **Generate Starter World** creates a new vault with those entries and connections, then selects the first entity. The chosen appearance is applied to the workspace and can be changed later in Settings.

When AI is enabled and available, the Oracle writes the entries. Otherwise the built-in local generator does. Quick Start creates the new vault and its content when you choose **Generate Starter World**; review the preview and premise first. This action is different from switching modes or asking Cif a question.

In a shared or read-only vault, use the views available to you. Changing the tool selection does not grant permission to edit that vault.
