## Create a Tome or Forbidden Book

Open **Generators** from the activity bar and choose **Tome & Forbidden Book**. Set its setting, book type, physical format, rarity, custodian, primary reading hazard, and language. Add campaign context to connect its history and clues to your world, or leave options open for the generator to choose.

The draft includes a physical description, provenance and transmission history, contents and revelations, study risks, adventure hooks, and GM reference notes. Review the claims and adapt details to fit your campaign before keeping them.

With **AI Disabled**, the generator uses a local template and needs no AI key or network request. It still uses the selected setting, era, format, custodian, language, and primary hazard, though its prose is more structured than an AI draft.

The result stays a draft until you choose **Open in Editor**. Review and edit it there, then use **Apply Changes** to keep it or **Discard** to remove it.
