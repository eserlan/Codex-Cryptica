# Session Hub

The Session Hub is the list on the random generators page where your generated drafts collect while you work.

## What goes in it

Every draft you generate appears in the Session Hub automatically, with the newest at the top. Nothing is saved to your vault until you choose to save it.

## What you can do with a draft

- **Reuse it as context** so the next thing you generate fits the same world.
- **Refine it** to ask for a focused change, then review the new version before you accept it.
- **Share it** as a public snapshot. Your original draft stays private.
- **Save it to Codex** when you want to keep it. Pick which drafts to save; unticked drafts are left behind.

## Starting over

Use **Clear** in the Session Hub to empty the list. Saved entities in your vault are not affected.
