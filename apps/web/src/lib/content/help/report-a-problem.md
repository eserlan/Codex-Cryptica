If something in Codex Cryptica isn't working, or you have an idea, you can send it straight to the maintainer from the app. You don't need a GitHub or Discord account, and you don't need to talk to Cif.

## How to open the form

Use **Report a problem** in any of these places:

- the header of the Help window
- the Help tab in Settings
- the footer of the Cif help panel
- the header of the campaign generator
- the footer of the public website, including the public generator and tool pages

## What you can send

Choose one of three kinds:

- **Something isn't working**: say what you were trying to do and what happened.
- **I have an idea**: describe what would help.
- **Something else**: anything that isn't one of the above.

The description can be up to 4,000 characters. You can also add an email address if you want a reply. It's optional.

## What is sent

Before you press **Send report**, the form lists exactly what will be sent:

- the app version
- the part of the app you were in, such as the tabletop, the generators or the help window
- your browser (for example Firefox or Safari), and whether your screen is narrow or wide

Nothing from your campaign is sent. That includes your entities, notes, lore, chat, AI responses, images and file names. Recognisable access keys in your text are removed before the report is stored.

## Who can read it

Each report is stored as a private issue on GitHub. Only the maintainer and people they explicitly grant access to can read it. Reports are never published. If the maintainer turns a report into a public issue, they write a short summary by hand.

If you give an email address, it is stored with your report and used only to reply to it.

## How long it's kept

Reports are deleted after 180 days. If you want one deleted sooner, quote its reference to the maintainer and it will be removed.

## When it doesn't send

- **Offline**: the form says so. Your text is kept, so you can send it once you're back online.
- **Too many reports**: you can send up to 5 reports an hour and 20 a day. The form tells you when to try again.
- **Service problem**: your text is kept. You can try again, or report on Discord or GitHub instead.

Every report gets a reference such as `CC-7F3K-9Q2M` when it's received. Keep it if you want to ask about the report later.
