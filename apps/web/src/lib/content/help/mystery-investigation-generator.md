## Create a Mystery & Investigation

Open **Campaign Generators** and choose **Mystery & Investigation**. Choose a genre, tone, case type, scope, complexity, clue density and pressure, then generate the case in one go. Add campaign context to connect it to your setting. The default design is **Fixed Truth**: the responsible person, motive, means and timeline are decided before the suspects and clues are written.

The staged run builds the truth, cast and locations, clue network, pressure and outcomes, then checks the assembled case. You can cancel while it runs. Each essential revelation is meant to have two evidence routes from different sources, so one missed clue does not stop the investigation. Review the draft for fit with your table before play.

## Keep GM truth private

The draft separates player-facing case material in **Content** from the solution, actual timeline, suspect knowledge, clue index and outcomes in **Lore**. Use **Copy Player Handout** for the spoiler-safe Content-only export and share that with players; keep Lore in your GM notes. **Copy GM Case File** includes both Content and Lore. Check the saved entity before exporting or sharing it, especially if your vault workflow includes Lore.

## AI and offline use

With AI enabled, the case is built in stages so later parts use the accepted facts and names from earlier parts. If a stage cannot produce a usable result after a retry, the generator tries one-pass generation; if that also fails, it creates a local draft. With AI Disabled, it uses a local template without a network request; expect a simpler case that you should expand before running.

The result remains a draft until you choose the available save or apply action. Review and refine it, then keep it in your vault or discard it.
