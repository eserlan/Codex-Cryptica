# Export and Back Up Your Vault

Your vault lives in your browser, so a copy outside the browser is the safest protection against a cleared browser, a lost device or a mistake. The simplest copy is a **Portable Backup**: your whole vault in one file you can keep anywhere.

## Download a Portable Backup

1. Open **Settings → Vault**.
2. Find **Portable Backup**.
3. Click **Export Backup**.

Your browser downloads one archive file. A message tells you how many files went into it. This works in every browser and needs no folder permission.

## Restore a Portable Backup

1. Open **Settings → Vault**.
2. Under **Portable Backup**, click **Import Backup**.
3. Choose the archive file.

Restoring always creates a **new vault** with the name stored in the archive and switches to it. It never overwrites or merges into the vault you are in, so it is safe to try.

## Which copy should I use?

| You want to…                                          | Use                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Keep a file you control, in any browser               | **Portable Backup** (this article)                                            |
| Keep your vault mirrored in a folder on your computer | **Save to Folder**, see Offline Support & Local Folders                       |
| Keep an automatic off-device copy                     | **Cloud copy**, see Codex Cryptica Cloud Backup or Google Drive sync          |
| Show your world to other people                       | **Publishing**. This is not a backup, it makes a public copy of what you pick |
| Bring a few files from another vault or a folder in   | **Import Files**, see Importing Notes                                         |
| Reuse one entity in another vault                     | **The Shelf**                                                                 |

## Good habits

- Export before big changes, such as a large import or a merge.
- Keep at least one copy outside this device.
- A backup file holds your vault data. Treat it as private and store it like any other personal file.
