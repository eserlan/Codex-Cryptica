## Create an urban fantasy faction

Open the [Urban Fantasy Faction Generator](/generators/urban-fantasy-faction). Choose a mode, faction type, operating scope and moral posture, then add optional campaign context. Generate a modern supernatural group with city resources, an agenda, danger, notable NPCs, internal pressure, a rival and a table-ready hook.

The result is one faction operating inside a city or network, not a generated city. It is system-agnostic and does not claim official support from any third-party game.

## AI and local generation

AI is optional. With AI disabled or unavailable, the page creates a faction from local tables without an account or network request. AI generation uses the selected options and optional campaign context to write a more detailed draft.

## Save the faction to Codex

Choose **Save to Codex** to hand the faction draft to Codex Cryptica in the same browser. Open the app to review it in your local vault. The generated faction stays a draft until you choose to keep it.
