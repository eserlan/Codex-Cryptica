# The Last Grind

<!-- source-id: ros-overview -->

A one-shot for a small party, playable in about 30 turns and usable with any rules. The flour mill at Greywater Bend has stopped, the village is hungry, and the miller has vanished. The party must find out why, and decide what happens to the mill.

## Scene 1: The Ferry Landing

<!-- source-id: ros-scene-1-landing -->

> **Read aloud:** The ferry bumps against a rotting jetty. Across the reeds a tall stone mill stands silent, its great wheel hanging still above brown water. A woman in a patched coat waits on the planks with her arms folded.

Maren Holt meets the party here and asks them to restart the mill before the village runs out of bread. She will answer questions but steers away from questions about her father.

- **Scene condition:** If the party arrives after dark, Maren is absent and Scene 2 begins with the gate already open.
- The reed path to the left of the jetty leads to the optional branch.

##Scene 2: The Mill Yard

<!-- source-id: ros-scene-2-yard -->

> **Read aloud:** Sacks lie split open across the yard, spilling grey powder. A cart has been dragged through the mud and abandoned. Beside the mill door hangs a ledger on a nail, its pages swollen with damp.

The ledger is the first way to learn that the sluice was sabotaged: one page records a lock fitted to the sluice pin the week before the miller vanished. A party that ignores the ledger can still learn the same thing in the cellar (Scene 4).

> **GM only:** The sluice pin was filed through from the inside by Tobiah Reed, so that the wheel would jam and the mill would stop.

- **Scene condition:** If the party makes noise in the yard, Wick the dog-handler arrives from the wheel room within a few turns and the encounter in Scene 3 begins on alert.

## Scene 3: The Wheel Room

<!-- source-id: ros-scene-3-wheel-room -->

> **Read aloud:** Inside, the wheel shaft groans against a jammed gear. Two lanterns burn low. A broad man stands beside the sluice lever with a heavy cudgel, and a wolfhound growls at his heel.

Wick guards the sluice lever. The party must free the gear, which needs the lever pulled. There are two ways past Wick.

- **By force:** Wick and his hound fight until one of them is down or the party retreats. Wick yields if his hound is hurt.
- **By talk:** If a character names Tobiah's debt or shows Wick the filed pin, he lowers his cudgel and steps aside. He would rather lose his wages than face a charge of ruining the mill.

> **GM only:** Wick is owed three months of pay by Tobiah and will switch sides if he hears that Tobiah never meant to pay him.

- **Scene condition:** Once the lever is pulled, the wheel turns and the water in the cellar starts to rise. The party has about five turns to finish the cellar.

## Optional Branch: The Reed Shrine

<!-- source-id: ros-branch-shrine -->

> **Read aloud:** A ring of leaning stones stands in the reeds, a clay lamp set in the centre. Fresh bread has been left beside it.

This side path can be skipped with no effect on the ending. If the party visits, the lamp gives a calm blessing: the next roll to calm someone, to persuade someone or to resist fear in this adventure may be repeated once. The shrine also holds a hint toward the cellar.

> **GM only:** The shrine's keeper is long dead. Maren leaves the bread there in her father's memory.

## Scene 4: The Miller's Cellar

<!-- source-id: ros-scene-4-cellar -->

> **Read aloud:** A narrow stair drops into a cold cellar. Water laps at the bottom step. A chair is bolted to the floor, and a thin man sits in it, bound and gagged, blinking at the lantern light.

The miller, Aldous Holt, is found here, alive. If the party did not read the ledger, they find a second copy of the same record in his coat: the receipt for the lock on the sluice pin. This is the second way to learn about the sabotage.

> **GM only:** Aldous hid in the cellar himself. Tobiah did not take him, and he is bound by his own hand to protect his daughter from a charge of theft.

- **Scene condition:** If the party stays more than five turns, the water reaches the ceiling and the party must leave without the miller or swim out at a cost.
- **Scene condition:** If the party arrives with Wick as an ally, Aldous speaks freely.

## Cast

<!-- source-id: ros-cast -->

The people the party can meet.

#### Maren Holt

<!-- source-id: ros-npc-maren -->

Maren is the miller's daughter, tired and quick to anger. **Motivation:** Maren wants the mill running again to pay off her late mother's debt before the bailiff comes.

### Tobiah Reed

<!-- source-id: ros-npc-tobiah -->

Tobiah is the mill's accountant, polite and unhurried. **Motivation:** Tobiah wants the mill to fail so the bailiff will sell it cheaply to him.

### Wick

<!-- source-id: ros-npc-wick -->

Wick is a dog-handler and hired guard, blunt and loyal to whoever pays. **Motivation:** Wick wants his unpaid wages and a safe road out of the valley.

## Ending

<!-- source-id: ros-ending -->

The adventure ends when the party leaves the mill. Two outcomes are possible, depending on what they did.

### Ending: The Mill Turns

<!-- source-id: ros-ending-restored -->

> **Read aloud:** The wheel turns, slow and heavy, and flour pours into the first sack. In the village, someone starts to cheer.

The sluice pin was exposed and Tobiah is named. The bailiff cannot sell the mill. Maren keeps her home and the village eats.

### Ending: The Water Takes It

<!-- source-id: ros-ending-flooded -->

> **Read aloud:** The cellar floods and the mill lies still. By morning the reeds have crept across the yard.

The party left without exposing Tobiah, or the cellar flooded with the miller inside. The bailiff sells the mill to Tobiah, and Maren leaves the bend for good.
