# The Silent Watchtower

<!-- source-id: tower-title -->

A tiny synthetic one-shot used only to build the example in the conversion prompt.

## Overview

<!-- source-id: tower-overview -->

A hillside watchtower has gone dark. Reeve Tomas, the worried village official, asks the party to relight the beacon before the next caravan crosses the valley.

## The Ridge Watchtower

<!-- source-id: tower-ridge -->

A stone signal tower stands above the valley road. A narrow path leads down from the tower to the valley road.

## The Valley Road

<!-- source-id: tower-road -->

A rutted track that caravans follow below the ridge. The path up to the watchtower starts here.

## The Cold Beacon

<!-- source-id: tower-beacon -->

The brazier at the top of the tower is unlit. It smells of fresh lamp oil, so someone tended it recently. The source does not say where the lamp oil is kept. If the party gets inside, play continues in Inside the Tower.

Villagers say a ghost puts the beacon out each night. This is not true.

## The Barred Door

<!-- source-id: tower-door -->

The tower door is barred from the inside. A Strength check (DC 14) forces it open. The party can also talk to the watchman inside and convince him to lift the bar.

## Inside the Tower

<!-- source-id: tower-inside -->

Old Hob, the watchman, huddles beside the cold stove and answers questions once the bar is lifted. He saw nothing.

## What Tomas Hid

<!-- source-id: tower-secret -->

Tomas put the beacon out himself to hide a smuggling run. Players do not learn this unless they find the ledger.

## Endings

<!-- source-id: tower-endings -->

If the party relights the beacon, the caravan passes safely.
