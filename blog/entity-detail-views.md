An entity is more than a note. Its detail view brings together the writing, relationships and campaign tools attached to a character, place, faction, event or other part of your world.

Open an entity from the explorer, graph, table or search results. The **detail sidebar** lets you inspect it while keeping your current workspace in view. For a larger editing space, select **Enter Zen Mode** in the sidebar header. On the Spatial Canvas, double-click an entity card to open it in Zen Mode.

The two views work with the same entity. Choose the sidebar when you are moving between entities or checking the graph; choose Zen Mode when you want more room to read, edit or explore the entity’s tabs.

## Read and edit the entity

The **Status** view is the entity’s home. It shows its title, category, Chronicle and Lore, along with relevant metadata such as labels, dates, aliases and connections. The exact fields depend on the entity type.

In your own vault, choose **Edit** to change the title, aliases, labels, dates or text. **Save Changes** keeps your edits; **Cancel** discards unsaved changes. Guest and demo vaults are read-only.

### Revise Chronicle and Lore with AI

Use **AI Revise Description** from the entity sidebar or Zen Mode when you want help improving an existing entry. Add instructions if you have a specific goal, such as preserving the facts while clarifying a motivation. Review the proposed Chronicle and Lore before applying it. **Apply Changes** saves the revision; **Discard** leaves the current writing in place. You can also review Lore changes before saving.

This is a draft-first workflow: generating a revision does not replace your text. AI features require an available AI provider and are unavailable in guest or demo vaults. See the [full revision steps](/help#help/creating-and-editing-entities).

## Work with relationships

The **Connections** section on Status shows entities directly linked to the current one. Use **+ Add** to choose another entity and describe the relationship. You can also edit or remove connections there.

The **Connections** tab gives you a focused, read-only map of direct links around this entity. Select a connected entity to open its details and follow the network one step at a time. For the full world graph and its layouts, return to Graph view.

If the Connections Proposer has found a possible relationship, its suggestions appear in the detail view. Review each one and use the checkmark to add the connection.

## Generate a related entity

On Status, choose **Generate Related** to start a generator with this entity and nearby connections as context. Select a generator, adjust its options, then review the proposed entity. You can choose whether it should link back to the source. Open the draft in the editor to review it, then apply or discard it.

The generated preview does not become part of your vault by itself. The [Generate Related guide](/help#help/generate-related) explains each step and the link option.

## Explore the other tabs

Tabs vary by entity type. When available, they add a focused view without creating a separate copy of the entity’s information.

- **Family** (characters): view relatives across generations using family connections.
- **Timeline**: see events directly connected to this entity, ordered by date. The view is read-only.
- **Map**: view or manage the entity’s location and map pins. A pin marks a position on a map; it does not create a relationship.
- **Stats**: view a character’s stat sheet and its associated controls.
- **Chats**: open configured guest chat for a character when you are viewing a shared world.

For details, see the [Family Tree](/help#help/family-tree), [Entity Timeline](/help#help/entity-timeline), [Map Mode](/help#help/map-mode), [Stat Sheets](/help#help/stat-sheets) and [Guest Character Chat](/help#help/guest-character-chat) guides.

## Use the entity header

The header keeps common actions close by:

- **Enter Zen Mode** opens the full-screen entity view from the sidebar.
- **Send to Shelf** copies an entity to the in-browser Shelf so you can import it into another vault in this browser. It does not remove or change the original.
- **Set parent** manages the entity hierarchy where available.
- **Edit** and **Delete** manage the current entity. Deletion requires confirmation and is permanent.

The available actions depend on your vault, the entity type and whether you have editing access. In shared or guest views, private editing actions are hidden.

## Choose the view for the task

Stay in the **sidebar** to check a connection, update a label or move quickly through your graph. Open **Zen Mode** to read a long Chronicle, revise Lore, inspect a family tree or work across several detail tabs. From either view, the same entity remains the source of truth for your campaign.

For more on creating and editing entities, see [Creating and Editing Entities](/help#help/creating-and-editing-entities). To learn the wider workspace, browse the [Codex Cryptica help guide](/help).
