## How Fit-for-Purpose Matching Works (Not by Filename)

Selection in [`matchArtwork()`](../packages/adventure-artwork-engine/src/matching.ts) **never inspects filenames** (filenames are content-addressed: `<id>-<variant>-<sha12>.webp`). Matching runs against structured asset metadata:

1. **Hard Eligibility Gates**:
   - `!withdrawn` and `review.status === "approved"`.
   - `asset.type` matches (`location` | `npc` | `scene` | `atmosphere`).
   - `asset.genres` includes the requested genre (`neutral` | `fantasy` | `contemporary` | `science-fiction` | `horror`, defaulting to `neutral`).
   - Zero overlap between `asset.excludes` and active cues.
2. **Minimum Suitability**:
   - At least **1** cue in `asset.primaryCues` must match `cues.primaryCues`; otherwise the UI falls back to text-only.
3. **Weighted Scoring & Deterministic Tie-Break**:
   - `score = primary * 8 + setting * 4 + mood * 1 + time * 1`
   - Equal scores break lexicographically by `asset.id`.
