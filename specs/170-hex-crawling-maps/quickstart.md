# Quickstart Guide: Hex Crawling Maps

This guide demonstrates how to configure and use the Hex Crawling system in Codex Cryptica.

## 1. Enabling a Hex Grid on an Overland Map

1. Open any campaign map or create a new blank overland map.
2. In the top map toolbar, toggle **Grid** on.
3. Open **Grid Settings** (the grid icon).
4. Under **Grid Type**, choose:
   - **Hex (Pointy)**: Vertical columns with points facing top/bottom (standard for many overland hex maps).
   - **Hex (Flat)**: Horizontal rows with flat edges on top/bottom.
5. Adjust **Grid Cell Size** to align with the scale of your map art.
6. Optional: Toggle **Show Hex Coordinates** to display axial coordinates (`01.04`, `02.04`) in cell centers.
7. Click **Apply to All** or **Close**.

## 2. Snapping Tokens to Hex Centers

1. Drag any party token, monster encounter, or NPC token onto the map.
2. When released, the token automatically centers on the nearest hex.
3. Right-click a token $\to$ **Resize** to scale the token to 1×, 2×, or 3× hex diameter.

## 3. Revealing Hex Fog of War

1. Enable **Fog of War** in the GM toolbar.
2. Select the **Hex Reveal** tool from the fog tools.
3. Click any shrouded hex to reveal its exact hexagonal area without ragged brush borders.
4. Alt-click (or choose **Hex Hide**) to re-shroud a hex in fog.
5. In **Token Vision Mode**, moving the party token will automatically reveal a radius of hexes based on the party's vision range.

## 4. Measuring Overland Travel Distance

1. Select the **Measurement Tool** (Ruler).
2. Click and drag between two hexes.
3. The measurement line snaps between hex centers and displays the exact discrete hex distance:
   - Example: `4 hexes (24 mi)` when configured for 6 miles per hex.
