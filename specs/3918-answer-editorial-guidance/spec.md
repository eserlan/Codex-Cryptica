# Feature: Reader-first Answer authoring guidance

Source: https://github.com/eserlan/Codex-Cryptica/issues/3918

## Scope

Improve drafting instructions and remove conflicting structural test policy. Preserve the existing post-merge editorial correction loop. No new reviewer service, publishing gate, public URL, bulk article rewrite, or schema variant.

## Reader outcomes and requirements

1. Writers declare the reader level and job before selecting a structure: beginner rules, GM technique, comparison, or definition/reference. Existing blocks remain supported; examples, checklists and product sections are chosen for usefulness.
2. Mechanical demonstrations show inputs, calculation, outcome and next action. Narrative demonstrations show a situation, intervention and player-visible consequence. Before/after and a concluding takeaway are optional.
3. A cross-section editing pass removes duplicated claims across the excerpt, opening, headings, example, checklist and ending without losing useful distinctions.
4. Rules use authoritative references and explicit version boundaries where differences matter; abbreviations are introduced and listed bonuses are not counted twice. An example sequence must not become a universal rule.
5. Links support the reader's next job. Prioritise cornerstone and relevant neighbouring Answers, consider visible links for meaningful discovery overlaps, and allow zero tools or product promotion.
6. Retain #3035 principles, British English, factual restraint, discovery ownership, images, link validation and existing automated editorial checks. Do not add style blacklists, word-count goals or mechanical editorial-quality scores.
7. Synchronise canonical and compatible skill entry points without dropping newer guidance.
8. Demonstrate effectiveness against exact #3899 and #3915 snapshots and at least two different additional Answer types. Record preventable defects separately from facts and product claims requiring independent verification.

## Acceptance scenarios

- A beginner can use a listed +5 attack bonus, d20 result 12 and target AC 15 to obtain 17 and a hit; a stated d8 result 6 plus 3 produces 9 damage. The example identifies the next action and does not imply all damage or healing starts with a d20.
- A concise definition may omit a dedicated example, checklist, tools and product section and still validate. An empty body, malformed block or broken supplied link still fails.
- A comparison supports choosing between options; a GM technique shows a consequence players can observe. Neither requires an artificial weak/strong pair.
- Compatibility copies expose the same editorial policy and preserve current operational safeguards.

## Delivery boundary

The implementation updates internal authoring guidance, tests and the editorial benchmark. Published Answer content, schema, renderer and the existing correction loop remain unchanged.
