# Authoring and compatibility contract

- Existing valid Answer inputs and block shapes remain valid.
- A definition may have one substantive prose section, no dedicated example/checklist, no codexConnection and no related tools. Omitted related arrays parse to empty arrays.
- Included examples may be walkthroughs, worked scenes or comparisons; no required weak/strong item names or takeaway paragraph.
- Empty body, malformed blocks, invalid supplied links and malformed tables continue to fail existing validation.
- Related-Answer integrity, discovery ownership, SEO, R2 and deterministic editorial rules remain enforced. Do not add a quota of product links.
- Canonical and compatibility skills expose the same policy after synchronisation. Preserve existing art direction, category coverage and operational verification guidance.
- No new API, public route or rendering order. Product material remains optional and after the substantive answer.
